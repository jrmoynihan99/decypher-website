## Q: Should my W-9 have my Social Security number or my LLC's EIN? (F46)

**Short answer:** If your LLC is taxed as an S-Corp, your W-9 uses the business name and its EIN.

How to fill it out:

| Your setup | Line 1 | Number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single-member LLC, not an S-Corp | Your legal name | Your SSN |
| LLC taxed as an S-Corp | Your LLC's name | Your LLC's EIN |

> Hi [name], thank you for your help with my taxes.

*These are not guarantees. Every creator's situation is different.*

## Q: What can I write off as a content creator? (F19)

**Short answer:** Creators can generally write off expenses that are ordinary and necessary for their content business.

- **Equipment.** Cameras, lenses, lighting.
- **Meals.** Usually 50% when business is discussed.
